Travel Explorer - Site Plan

Site Name

Travel Explorer

This name was chosen because it accurately represents the purpose of the website: to explore and discover top tourist destinations worldwide. It's catchy, easy to remember, and conveys a sense of adventure and discovery.

Site Purpose

Travel Explorer is an interactive travel guide that showcases top tourist destinations around the world. The site aims to inspire travelers, provide valuable information about various locations, and allow users to save their favorite destinations for future reference. Key features include:

  • A curated list of popular travel destinations with detailed descriptions and high-quality images
  • Interactive elements such as a featured destinations carousel
  • A "Save to Favorites" feature using localStorage for personalized user experience
  • Responsive design for seamless browsing across different devices
  • Newsletter subscription for travel updates and tips

Scenarios

  1. As a potential traveler, what are the top-rated destinations I should consider for my next vacation?
  2. How can I save information about interesting destinations to review later when planning my trip?
  3. What kind of activities and attractions are available at a specific destination?
  4. How can I stay updated with the latest travel tips and destination recommendations?

Color Schema

  • Primary Color (Sky Blue - #3498db): Used for headings and primary buttons
  • Secondary Color (Orange - #f39c12): Used for accents, highlights, and call-to-action elements
  • Text Color (Dark Blue - #2c3e50): Used for body text and content
  • Background Color (Light Gray - #ecf0f1): Used for the main background
